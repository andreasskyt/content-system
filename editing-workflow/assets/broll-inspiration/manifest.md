# B-Roll Inspiration Library

Descriptions of each image in this folder. Claude reads this manifest to pick
the right image for each b-roll moment — only selected images are loaded and viewed.

## dashboard1.png
Revenue analytics dashboard with purple/blue bar charts showing monthly revenue by sales channel, donut charts showing revenue breakdown ($5,969,673), and a line chart for revenue by day of week. Clean grid layout on white background. Use for: revenue, sales data, analytics, monthly performance.

## dashboard2.png
Financial KPI dashboard showing Total Accounts Receivable ($6,621,280), Accounts Payable ($1,630,270), Equity Ratio (75.38%), Debt Equity (1.10%). Includes gauge charts, line graphs for working capital, and stacked bar chart for profit & loss. Use for: financial metrics, accounting, business health, KPIs.

## blue-upwards-trending-grapgh.png
Neon blue glowing bar chart on dark navy grid background, bars increasing left to right with a sweeping upward arrow. Futuristic, tech-forward style. Use for: growth, scaling, revenue increase, positive trends, going up.

## blue-upward-trending-graph-2.png
Teal/cyan stock-chart style line graph on dark blue background with grid lines, showing upward trend line with price markers (10.354 to 15.112). Financial market aesthetic. Use for: stock growth, investment returns, market trends, consistent upward performance.

## roadmap.png
Product development roadmap infographic with winding road through 6 phases: Ideation, Product Design, Market Research, Testing & Prototyping, Improvement & Refinement, Launch. Teal circular icons at each stage on white background. Use for: process, roadmap, strategy, phases, step-by-step, product development.
