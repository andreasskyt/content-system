# Tags & upload settings

Two deliverables live here: the tag list the skill generates, and the upload
checklist [YOUR_NAME] runs in YouTube Studio. Both ship on every video.

---

## Tags

YouTube's own hint in Studio says it: tags are a minor signal, useful mainly
for **commonly misspelled** terms. So generate them, keep them cheap, and never
spend real effort here — the title, thumbnail and first 30 seconds decide the
video.

Produce **10–12 tags**, comma-separated on one line, in `tags.md`:

- **3–4 topic tags** — what the video is about (`vibe coding`, `ai dashboard`)
- **3–4 tool/tech tags** — named tools that appear in the video (`lovable`,
  `supabase`, `database schema`)
- **1–2 category tags** — the business bucket (`ai automation agency`)
- **3 branded tags, always** — `[your_name] brand`, `brand systems`, `[BRAND_SLUG]`.
  These are the ones doing actual work: they catch branded searches, and
  "[Brand]" is misspelled constantly.

Never exceed ~12. Tag stuffing does nothing and dilutes the few that matter.
Stay under 500 characters total (Studio enforces this).

## Upload checklist

Deliver this as the last section of `titles.md` so [YOUR_NAME] can tick through it
in Studio. Ordered by how much each actually affects performance.

**Matters a lot**

1. **Proofread the title.** Read it out loud. Grammar errors in a title are
   the one packaging mistake that costs credibility rather than clicks.
   (2026-08-11: shipped "dashboards shows wrong data" to the edit screen.)
2. **Test & Compare** — load 3 of the 5 thumbnails, the three most *different*
   ones. Studio also A/B tests titles now; use it if the top 2 titles are
   genuinely different arguments rather than rewordings.
3. **Video language = English.** Defaults to unset. It drives caption quality
   and makes the video eligible for auto-translated titles and subtitles —
   free reach in non-English markets.
4. **Add to a playlist.** Unset by default. Playlists drive session time,
   which is the metric that decides whether YouTube keeps promoting the video.
5. **Upload a real subtitle file** rather than leaning on auto-captions, when
   `/editing` has already produced one.

**Matters some**

6. **End screen** — point at the next video and the subscribe element.
7. **Pinned comment** carrying the booking link with `utm_medium=pinned_comment`
   so it's attributed separately from the description link.
8. **Category: Science & Technology.** Education is the alternative; either is
   defensible, just be consistent so the channel reads as one thing.

**Confirm these are set, then forget them**

- Made for kids: **No** — "yes" strips comments, notifications and
  personalised ads. It's the single most destructive wrong click on the page.
- AI use disclosure: **No** — accurate; the thumbnails are code and photos,
  and nothing in the video is synthetic. Answer honestly if that changes.
- Allow embedding: on · Publish to subscriptions feed: on
- Shorts remixing: allow — free distribution
- Comments: on, Basic moderation, sort by Top
- Automatic chapters: harmless to leave on; manual chapters in the description
  always win.
