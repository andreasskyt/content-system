# Style 4 — YouTube Promo

Promoting a newly posted YouTube video. Simple frame: real photo + short native-text copy + the video thumbnail as a rounded attachment. [YOUR_NAME] adds the link sticker manually in the app — the link IS the CTA, so the image carries **no CTA text**.

## Font
- Family: `'Helvetica Neue', -apple-system, sans-serif` (IG Classic), weight 700
- Sizes (540×960 design frame): header pill 21-22px · body pills 19-20px

## Text style
- Native per-line pills only (Style 1 mechanics: radius 6px, padding 5px 9px, `box-decoration-break: clone`).
- Header: short status line ("new video just went live"), white pill, top.
- Body: 2-3 lines on WHY to watch — curiosity or pain-hook, never a summary of the video. Black pills.
- NO CTA text, no "link below", no arrow to the sticker. NO emojis, no tilt.
- Emphasis (optional): one red `#ED2121` word.

## Colours
| Element | Combo |
|---|---|
| Header | white pill `#fff` / `#111` |
| Body | black pill `rgba(10,10,10,0.96)` / `#fff` |

## Thumbnail attachment
- The actual video thumbnail from `Marketing/Thumbnails/`, 16:9, centered horizontally.
- Width 440px (50px side margins), `border-radius: 16px`, shadow `0 8px 30px rgba(0,0,0,0.45)`.
- Vertical position: upper-middle (top ~170-220). May deliberately cover distracting photo elements (strangers' faces, clutter).

## Placement
- Header pill: top, y ~84.
- Thumbnail: y ~175-420.
- Body pills: directly under the thumbnail, y ~460-540, centered.
- Bottom half stays clean — that's where [YOUR_NAME] places the link sticker in the app.

## Photo
- Casual real-life work/travel shot (train, café, desk) — "just posted this from wherever I am" energy. No darkening needed unless text lacks contrast.
