# Style 2 — Plain Classic

The rant / text-wall style. Storytelling frames, day-to-day thoughts, pain narratives. White IG Classic text straight on the photo, no pills.

## Font
- Family: `'Helvetica Neue', -apple-system, sans-serif`
- Weight: 600 · line-height: 1.6
- Sizes (540×960 design frame): body 18-20px · optional one-line kicker 20-21px

## Text style
- Plain white text with subtle shadow: `color:#fff; text-shadow: 0 1px 4px rgba(0,0,0,0.55)`
- Left-aligned paragraphs, blank line between thoughts. Reads like typed-in-the-app rambling, structured underneath (pain → twist → point).
- A single white-pill header line (Style 1 white combo) MAY sit above the text wall ("Here's what it looks like inside:").
- Emphasis: white hand-drawn scribble underline under the key phrase (SVG, 4px stroke). No red in this style — red on photo without a pill looks fake.
- NEVER: emojis · tilted text · center-justified text walls

## Colours
- Text: white `#fff` only.
- Photo must be dark enough to carry white text: pick a moody photo or apply `brightness(0.7-0.8)`.

## Placement
- Text wall: top half, y 90-460, left margin 40px, right margin 60px.
- Keep text off [YOUR_NAME]'s face and the photo subject — the photo choice must have dark negative space (dark gym, night, wall, ceiling).
- Bottom 200px stays clear (photo breathes + reply bar).
