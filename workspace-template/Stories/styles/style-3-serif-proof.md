# Style 3 — Serif Proof

The client-wins / behind-the-scenes style. Testimonials, client meetups, day-in-the-life with an angle, proof frames with screenshots. Mimics IG's **Editor/Elegant** serif with brand-coloured pills.

## Font
- Family: `Georgia, 'Times New Roman', serif` (closest match to IG's Editor serif)
- Weight: 400 · line-height: 1.7
- Sizes (540×960 design frame): 18-20px. This style is always small and understated — big serif text looks designed, small serif text looks personal.

## Text style
- Per-line background pills, same mechanics as Style 1 (`box-decoration-break: clone`, radius 6px, padding 5px 9px).
- Voice: lowercase, casual, diary-like ("spent the afternoon with marco…"). No hard selling — the mechanism gets the credit ("his [your_offer] now…").
- Optional tag line at top: `[Client wins]` in a green pill, centered.
- Emphasis: rarely needed. If used: hand-drawn white underline. No red in this style.
- NEVER: emojis · tilted text · hype language, exclamation marks

## Colours (pill background / text) — the only combos allowed
| Combo | Background | Text | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Beige | `#F5F1EA` | `#303b2f` | main narration lines |
| Green | `#303b2f` | `#F5F1EA` | tag line, closing line, key claim |

## Placement
- Tag `[Client wins]`: top center, y ~90.
- Narration block: y 150-230, centered or left, 44px margins.
- Closing/claim block: lower third, y 690-760, above reply-bar zone.
- Faces stay visible — this style lives on people photos (client meetups, events). Text goes above heads and below chests.
- Proof screenshots (real Slack/DM/dashboard captures only — never fabricated): straight, not tilted, max 70% frame width, mid-frame, optional white hand-drawn arrow from text to screenshot.
