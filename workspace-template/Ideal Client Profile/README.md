# Ideal Client Profile

The single source for WHO you sell to and the exact words they use. Every scripting, carousel, and ad skill loads this folder before writing.

Minimum viable ICP (one file, `icp.md`):
- Who: industry, size, revenue band ([ICP_QUALIFIER]), role of the buyer.
- Top 5 pains, each with 2–3 verbatim quotes from real conversations.
- Top 5 desired outcomes, verbatim.
- Objections you hear on calls, verbatim, with your answer.
- Buying triggers (what happened right before they reached out).
- Words they use / words they never use.

Best source: transcripts of your own sales and discovery calls. If you use Fathom, the `/fathom-content-ideas` skill mines calls into content ideas; the same transcripts feed this file.
