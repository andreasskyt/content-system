# [YOUR_NAME] — tone of voice

Every skill reads this file before writing anything in your voice. Build it once from real material, then keep it short.

## How to build this file (30 minutes)
1. Collect 3–5 transcripts of you talking naturally: YouTube videos, sales calls, voice notes.
2. Ask Claude Code: "Read these transcripts and write my tone-of-voice sheet: sentence length, favourite phrases, words I never use, how I open, how I close, how I handle claims and numbers, level of formality, swearing, humour."
3. Edit the result by hand. Delete anything that isn't really you.

## Template (replace everything below)

**Sentence rhythm:** short, declarative. One idea per sentence. Occasional long sentence to explain a mechanism.
**Register:** direct, peer-to-peer, no hype. Talks to one person.
**Signature phrases:** "…", "…", "…"
**Never says:** "game-changer", "unlock", "supercharge", emoji strings, hashtags.
**Claims:** always specific numbers with context. Never round up. Never invent.
**Openers:** state the problem in the reader's words. No "Hey guys".
**Closers:** one clear next step. Comment keyword CTA or "watch the full video".
**Formatting rules:** no em-dashes (use a period or a comma), no headers in captions, no bullet walls in captions.
