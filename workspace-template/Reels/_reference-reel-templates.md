9. **What I Wish I Knew** — "if I could go back…" → the mistake → the lesson → apply-it CTA

11. **The Authority Bridge** — blending in as a commodity → the copying problem → build the unique bridge (expertise + story) → premium positioning → CTA
12. **The Inbound Flip** — myth: you must chase → cost of chasing → flip to attraction → the method → CTA
13. **The Unique Mechanism Finder** — stop copying → framework: core expertise, unique perspective, natural metaphor, differentiated delivery → CTA
